## 1. The App

- **Name:** Alpha Magic

- **Store link:** https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.inixrtechnology.alphamagic

- **What happens in the app:** ✨ AlphaMagic — Where the Alphabet Comes Alive in AR



AlphaMagic brings the alphabet to life through augmented reality, designed for kids ages 3-8. Each letter from A to Z transforms into a cinematic 3D scene right in your room — aeroplanes circling overhead, boats sailing across water, dinosaurs roaring in cages. With clear voice pronunciation, drag-and-pinch controls, and a magical Zoo Safari finale that unlocks after visiting every animal, AlphaMagic turns letter learning into an adventure kids beg to repeat.



🎓 HOW IT WORKS

1. Tap any letter on the home screen

2. Hear the letter and word ("A for Aeroplane")

3. Watch the 3D AR scene appear in your real room

4. Drag with one finger to spin, pinch with two to zoom

5. Tap the back button to return to the alphabet
