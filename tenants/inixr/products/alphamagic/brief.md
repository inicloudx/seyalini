_Every agent reads this before working. Facts come from the Google Play listing and the founder (Sep 2026). Items marked [FILL] still need the founder._

## 1. The App
- **Name:** AlphaMagic (store title "Alpha Magic"), by Team INIXR / INIXR Technology
- **Store link:** https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.inixrtechnology.alphamagic
- **One-line pitch:** Where the alphabet comes alive in AR: each letter from A to Z becomes a cinematic 3D scene right in your room.
- **How it works:** 1) tap a letter on the home screen · 2) hear the letter and word ("A for Aeroplane") · 3) the 3D AR scene appears in the real room · 4) drag with one finger to spin, pinch with two to zoom · 5) back button returns to the alphabet
- **The 26 letter scenes (use exactly these words, never others):** A for Aeroplane · B for Boat · C for Chameleon · D for Dinosaur · E for Elephant · F for Fish · G for Gorilla · H for Hero · I for Ice Dragon · J for Jellyfish · K for King Cobra · L for Lion · M for Megalodon · N for Ninja · O for Octopus · P for Penguin · Q for Queen Parrot · R for Rocket · S for Spaceship · T for Train · U for Unicorn · V for Volcano · W for Wizard · X for X-Ray · Y for Yak · Z for Zoo
- **Scenes are cinematic:** full ~90-second experiences, e.g. aeroplanes circling overhead, boats sailing across water, dinosaurs roaring in cages
- **Zoo Safari finale** unlocks after visiting every animal (gamified learning)
- **Key features:** clear voice pronunciation for every letter · 3-screen onboarding · parental gate before any purchase · resume-aware downloads · works offline once letters are downloaded
- **Safe for families:** zero ads · zero personal data · no social features or chat · no location tracking · camera used only in real time for AR (never recorded) · COPPA and Google Families Policy compliant

## 2. Audience
- **Primary:** Kids aged 3–8, with parents guiding them (parents decide the install), worldwide
- **Secondary (B2B):** Preschools and primary school teachers
- **Language of videos:** English

## 3. Why parents install
- Screen time that teaches letters, phonics and vocabulary, not just entertainment
- Safe: no ads, no data collection, offline, so a parent can hand over the phone with confidence
- Kids stay engaged because they *move* the 3D scenes themselves (spatial awareness, hands-on play)
- Big "wow" moments: a dinosaur, a megalodon or a rocket appearing in the living room
